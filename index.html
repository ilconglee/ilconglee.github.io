Wonbin Lee - - - - -
Wonbin Lee is a sound artist and composer. Textiles serve as a language that runs throughout her practice. Finding musical material in women's domestic labor—from the Korean fulling stone (dadeumitdol) to weaving and the sewing machine—she uses sound to make perceptible the time and traces that often go unnoticed in everyday life.
